We are pleased to share that the MOTUM paper has been accepted for publication in Imaging Neuroscience on 7 January 2026.

MOTUM is a system for Motion Online Tracking Under MRI that enables real-time motion tracking during functional MRI experiments, allowing researchers to combine naturalistic movement with neuroimaging in a controlled and flexible setup.
