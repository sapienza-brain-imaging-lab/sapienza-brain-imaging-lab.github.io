I’m a neuropsychologist and psychotherapist, actually working with individuals affected by brain injuries and psychological distress. Neuropsychological testing is my daily bread. My research interests include sense of agency and ownership, body representations, and body schema with a particular focus on anarchic hand syndrome. 
When I’m not writing clinical reports or designing cognitive exercises, I enjoy drawing, thrifting, cooking, traveling to northern countries, and cuddling my cat, Zorba.
